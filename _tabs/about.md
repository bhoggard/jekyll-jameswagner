James Wagner lives in New York and writes about art and politics on [jameswagner.com](/). He is the editor, along with [Barry Hoggard](https://bloggy.com), of the arts calendar [ArtCat](https://calendar.artcat.com).

For a longer biographical narrative, see [this page](/more-about.html).
